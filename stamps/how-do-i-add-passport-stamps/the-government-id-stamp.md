# ☑️ The Government ID Stamp

Prove you're a real, unique person using a government-issued document. human.tech, by the Holonym Foundation, transforms sensitive personal data into cryptographic proofs using zero-knowledge technology — privacy-preserving, compliance-ready identity verification.

## Three ways to verify

| Option | Cost | Proof | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Government ID** | $5 | On-chain SBT, reusable | Drivers' licenses, ID cards, residence permits, or passports without NFC |
| **NFC Passport** | $3 | On-chain SBT, reusable | Biometric passport — permanent, portable proof |
| **NFC Passport (Free)** | Free for a one-time, 1-week proof. $3 for the reusable on-chain version. | 1-week off-chain attestation | One-time verification, no ongoing reuse needed |

## Prerequisites

* A smartphone with internet
* An Ethereum wallet
* A Human Passport account
* For **Government ID**: a valid ID document and your phone's camera
* For **NFC Passport** (Free or $3): an NFC-enabled smartphone + a biometric passport
* Wallet balance to cover gas + the verification fee ($5, $3, or $0)

## Step-by-step

### 1. Open the Gov ID picker

Sign in at [app.passport.xyz](https://app.passport.xyz/), find the **Government ID** stamp card, click **Connect**.

You'll see the three options above. Pick one.

### 2. Complete the verification

**Government ID ($5):** Follow the prompts to photograph your ID and take a selfie. Confirm payment. Mint your Soulbound Token (SBT).

**NFC Passport ($3 or Free):** Open the ZKPassport app on your phone. Scan the NFC chip in your biometric passport. The proof generates locally — nothing leaves your device.

* **$3:** confirm payment, mint your SBT. Permanent, reusable across human.tech.
* **Free:** receive a 1-week off-chain attestation. No payment, one-time use.

### 3. Claim the Stamp on Human Passport

Back on your Passport dashboard, click **Check Eligibility** on the Government ID Stamp card. The button updates from "Connect" to "Verified."

## A note on the Free option

The Free option gives you a **1-week off-chain attestation**, useful when you need to prove humanity once. After 1 week, the attestation expires. For a permanent, reusable Stamp, use the **$3** on-chain option.
