# HUMN campaige update: Season 1 -> Season2

## Season 1 has ended

[HUMN Season 1](../humn-points-program/welcome-to-humn-onchain-sumr-season-1-finished.md) is now complete. The season focused on verifying unique human participation using Human Passport.

### Season 1 at a glance

• 68,838 unique humans participated

• 38,599 Human Passports were minted onchain

• Passports were minted across Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Linea, zkSync, and Scroll

• Onchain Passport scores are available to partners directly onchain, without API integration

## HUMN Season 2 is live

[HUMN Season 2](https://passport.human.tech/blog/humn-season-2-manifest-your-humanity) has launched. This season shifts the focus from verification to participation and contribution across the human.tech ecosystem.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-01-16 at 12.17.35.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### How participation works in Season 2

• Verified humans can sign the Covenant of Humanistic Technologies

• Signing grants 1,000 HUMN Points

• Early signers may receive a limited-edition art NFT, subject to availability

• Participation includes contributing ideas, artifacts, and proposals, and engaging in future governance and grants processes
