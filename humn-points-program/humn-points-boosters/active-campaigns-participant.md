# Active Campaigns Participant

_Here comes the formerly announced Easter Egg:_&#x20;

You've earned additional HUMN Points for having **scored 20+** in **three or more partner campaigns** that were protected with Human Passport Stamps!

Some important things to keep in mind about this feature:

* The campaign must have been protected by the Passport Stamps product (ie, the Passport app), and not Passport Models or Data Services.&#x20;
* There was a snapshot taken on July 14th of all campaigns counting towards this reward.&#x20;

If eligible, you can see **500 HUMN Points** added to your HUMN score, with **Scored > 20 with 3 or more partner campaigns** as name in the score breakdown.&#x20;
