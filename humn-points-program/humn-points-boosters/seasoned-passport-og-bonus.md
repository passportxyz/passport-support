## FAQ

**Can I claim this bonus if I didn’t participate in 2023?**

No. This reward is exclusive to users who claimed an OAT during the 2023 Anti-Sybil Assembly campaign.

**Where can I check if I received the bonus?**

Open your [HUMN Points dashboard](https://app.human.tech) and look for “Seasoned Passport OG” or “The Chosen One” in your Score Breakdown (top right corner).

**Do I need to claim anything manually?**

No action is required. Eligible wallets automatically receive their points.

**I claimed multiple OATs — do I get multiple rewards?**

No. Each wallet is eligible for a single reward tier only.

**What if my wallet no longer has the OAT?**

As long as your wallet originally claimed the OAT, it remains eligible. The verification is based on claim history, not current ownership.
